## What "3x5" means

The sales page says to "prep dinner in three 5-minute steps", spread through
the day, and then the dinner cooks with little attention.

The Kit checkout page words it differently ("three simple steps, five
minutes"). Use the sales page's wording until Ali says which one is right.

## What's inside

- The 3x5 system: a repeatable way to prep in short bursts
- 12 family-friendly dinner recipes (four weeks of three dinners a week)
- Responsive feeding guidance in every recipe
- A weekly grocery list for each week
- Bonus: Picky Eating Roadmap Companion Guide
- Bonus: Scripts for Real-Life Mealtime Moments

## Who it's for (the sales page's own framing)

Parents who are drained by late afternoon, want simple family meals without
long weekend prep sessions, and want one meal that works for the whole
family. Stated benefits: "get your time back before dinner", "reduce
mealtime battles", "supports curiosity and exposure without pressure".

## What it is NOT: never claim these

The sales page is explicit, so the emails must be too. It is not gourmet
cooking, not a strict meal plan, not individualized nutrition counselling,
and it does **not guarantee kids will eat everything**. Never promise that
picky eating gets "fixed", that kids will "finally eat dinner", or any
outcome. No refunds are offered, so never write "risk-free" or
"money-back".
