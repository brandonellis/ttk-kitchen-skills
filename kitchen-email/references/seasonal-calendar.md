# Seasonal calendar - what fits each send date

The audience follows Canadian spelling and mostly a North American calendar,
so use Northern Hemisphere seasons. Pick recipes by the **send date** (the
Sunday), not the drafting date. Two weeks either side of a holiday counts as
"near" it.

## Month by month

| Month | Lean into | Hold back on |
|---|---|---|
| January | Fresh-start routines, freezer meals, soups and stews, oats, iron-rich comfort food, lunchbox restarts after the break | Holiday baking, summer produce |
| February | Cozy dinners, Valentine's treats (pink/red fruit, heart shapes), Family Day (Canada, third Monday) | Grilling, popsicles |
| March | Late-winter batch cooking, early spring green (peas, spinach), March break snacks | Pumpkin, heavy holiday dishes |
| April | Spring produce, Easter (eggs, carrot muffins), picnic-ready snacks | Pumpkin, gingerbread |
| May | Berries arriving, Mother's Day brunch, outdoor snacks, Victoria Day long weekend | Heavy stews |
| June | Strawberries, smoothies, picnic and road-trip food, end-of-school, Father's Day | Pumpkin, soups |
| July | Peak summer: popsicles, frozen yogurt bites, no-oven meals, zucchini, Canada Day | Ovens-on-for-an-hour dinners, pumpkin |
| August | Zucchini and corn, peaches, back-to-school prep starts | Holiday flavours |
| September | Back to school and lunchboxes, apples, freezer-friendly breakfasts, routines | Popsicles, ice cream |
| October | Pumpkin, apple, squash, sweet potato, cinnamon spice, Canadian Thanksgiving (second Monday), Halloween snacks | Ice cream, popsicles, summer berries as the star |
| November | Cozy dinners, slow cooker and sheet pan, US Thanksgiving (fourth Thursday), Black Friday, holiday prep starting | Popsicles, frozen treats |
| December | Holiday baking (gingerbread, cranberry, cinnamon), gifting snacks, school-break meals, make-ahead breakfasts for holiday mornings | Ice cream, popsicles, summer salads |

Spice flavours (pumpkin spice, cinnamon) belong to September-November;
gingerbread and cranberry belong to November-December.

## Picking 1-3 recipes

- **Seasonal fit first.** Every recipe has a reason it belongs this week:
  an in-season ingredient, a holiday, or the week's routine (lunchboxes in
  September, holiday mornings in December).
- **Useful for parents.** Favour make-ahead, freezer-friendly, one-pan,
  self-feeding, lunchbox-ready or iron-rich recipes.
- **Mix the meals.** Don't pick three muffins. Aim for a spread, like one
  breakfast or snack and one dinner.
- **Don't repeat.** Skip anything featured in the last 8 weeks of Kit
  broadcasts.
- **Count:** 2-3 by default. Use 1-2 when a sale section is in the email so
  the email stays short.

## Sale windows (when to add the 3x5 sale section)

| Window | Send dates that count |
|---|---|
| Black Friday / Cyber Monday | The Sunday before Black Friday and the Sunday of Black Friday weekend (2026: Nov 22 and Nov 29; 2027: Nov 21 and Nov 28) |
| New Year | Sundays from Dec 26 to Jan 15 |
| Back to school | Sundays from Aug 20 to Sep 10 |
| Anytime | Whenever Ali says there's a sale or a reason to promote |

For other years, Black Friday is the day after the fourth Thursday of
November and Cyber Monday is three days after that.
